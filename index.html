About the hotel

Welcome to Palema Crown Hotel

Palema Crown Hotel provides practical, comfortable accommodation for business travelers, NGO staff, researchers, government visitors, tourists and organizations working across Northern Uganda. Set directly along the busy Lira-Gulu Road in Library Ward, the hotel is easy to reach whether you are arriving from within Gulu City or travelling in from elsewhere in the region.

Our rooms, restaurant, bar and conference spaces are designed around one idea: give guests a dependable, welcoming base while they get on with the work, travel or occasion that brought them to Gulu. Every stay is supported by attentive, genuinely Ugandan hospitality.

Explore Gulu

Explore Gulu & Northern Uganda

Palema Crown Hotel provides a convenient base for visitors exploring Gulu City and Northern Uganda — from work at nearby institutions to time spent seeing the region.

Gulu City

Uganda's largest city in the north, and the region's commercial and administrative hub.

Gulu University

A leading academic institution in Northern Uganda, drawing students and researchers to the city.

St. Joseph's College Layibi

A well-known secondary school in Gulu, often visited by parents, alumni and partner organizations.

Local Markets

Bustling markets around the city center offering local produce, crafts and everyday goods.

Cultural Attractions

Sites and community spaces reflecting the Acholi heritage and history of the wider region.

Business District

Gulu's central commercial area, home to banks, offices and services for visiting professionals.
